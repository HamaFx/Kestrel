## 2. Industry Benchmark Synthesis & BiQuote Integration Blueprint

### 2.1 Lessons from Industry-Leading Engines

1. **[NautilusTrader](https://nautilustrader.io/docs/latest/concepts/backtesting/bar-execution/) (Bar-Based Execution & Parity)**:
   - **Adaptive Intrabar Path (`bar_adaptive_high_low_ordering`)**: When executing against OHLC bars, visiting `Open -> High -> Low -> Close` vs `Open -> Low -> High -> Close` based on proximity of `Open` to `High` vs `Low` (`|O - H| < |O - L|`) provides a deterministic, realistic intrabar path for resting orders.
   - **Order Type Fill Semantics**: Market and Stop-Market orders cross the book and pay `Ask + Slippage` (buy) / `Bid - Slippage` (sell). Resting Limit and Take-Profit orders provide liquidity and fill at their limit price (or bar open if gapped favourably) with **zero adverse slippage**.
   - **Causal Bar Timestamps**: A bar timestamped at `openTime` (`t`) is only observable at `t + barMs`.
2. **[QuantConnect LEAN](https://www.quantconnect.com/docs/v2/cloud-platform/live-trading/brokerages/quantconnect-paper-trading) (Brokerage Model & Reality Modeling)**:
   - **Unified Signal-to-Order Translation**: Backtesting and Paper Trading must share a single canonical signal-to-order translator so that limit prices, trailing stop modes (`atr`, `percent`, `fixed`), breakeven triggers (`breakEvenAtR`), time stops (`maxBars`), order expiry (`validBars`), and position flip rules behave identically in both environments.
   - **Market Hours & Stale Feed Protection**: A paper brokerage must never fill market orders or trigger stops against closed-market or stale quotes.
3. **[Freqtrade](https://www.freqtrade.io/en/stable/lookahead-analysis/) & [Jesse](https://python.financial/comparisons/freqtrade-vs-jesse/) (Anti-Lookahead & Statistical Rigor)**:
   - **Automated Prefix-Truncation Lookahead Test**: Chaining a full backtest against prefix-truncated backtests verifies that no feature or signal at bar `i` ever changes when bars `> i` are removed.
   - **Time-Series Annualized Sharpe/Sortino vs SQN**: Separate annualized equity-curve Sharpe/Sortino (computed from daily/bar returns scaled by $\sqrt{\text{periods/year}}$) from trade-count-scaled System Quality Number ($\text{SQN} = \frac{\mu_R}{\sigma_R}\sqrt{N}$), and penalize low-trade-count or high-drawdown combinations during parameter sweeps and walk-forward optimization.

### 2.2 BiQuote API (`https://biquote.io/docs/`) Integration Architecture

Per the official BiQuote documentation:

- **Ticks (`GET /api/{symbol}` & `GET /api/latest?symbols=A&symbols=B`)**:
  - Returns `{ symbol, bid, ask, mid, spread, last, volume, high, low, direction, dayDiffPercent, timestamp, source, marketState, stale, quoteAgeSeconds, lastQuoteAt }`.
  - `last` and `volume` are always `0` for FX/CFD; `bid` and `ask` carry the real live MT5 broker spread, and `mid` is the midpoint.
  - `marketState: "open" | "closed"`, `stale: boolean`, and `quoteAgeSeconds: number` are present on every response. Passing `allowStale=false` returns `404` when a quote is >5 minutes old.
  - **Fix in `@kestrel/data`**: Preserve `bid`, `ask`, `mid`, `spread`, `marketState`, `stale`, and `quoteAgeSeconds` through `ProviderPriceSnapshot` and `snapshotToTick` so Paper Trading uses real MT5 bid/ask spreads when `bid < ask` and rejects/defers fills when `marketState === 'closed'` or `stale === true`.
- **OHLC Candles (`GET /api/{symbol}/ohlc?interval=...&limit=1000&from=...&to=...`)**:
  - Returns `{ symbol, interval, bars: [{ openTime, open, high, low, close, volume, tickVolume, isOpen }] }` newest-first, up to `1000` bars per HTTP call.
  - **Fix 1 (Tick Volume Fallback)**: In `toCandleFromBiquote`, map `v: bar.volume > 0 ? bar.volume : (bar.tickVolume > 0 ? bar.tickVolume : null)` so volume-based indicators (`vwap`, `mfi14`, `obv`, `volumeSma20`) work on FX and Gold.
  - **Fix 2 (Multi-Chunk Pagination up to 5,000 Bars)**: When `count > 999` in `biquote.fetchOhlc` (and `binance.fetchCandles`), iteratively page backward using the oldest bar's `openTime - 1ms` as the `to` parameter (up to 5 chunks / 5,000 bars), deduplicating by `openTime` and sorting oldest-first.
  - **Fix 3 (Crypto Fallback Mapping)**: Map Kestrel's 6 crypto pairs (`BTCUSDT -> BTCUSD`, `ETHUSDT -> ETHUSD`, `SOLUSDT -> SOLUSD`, `BNBUSDT -> BNBUSD`, `XRPUSDT -> XRPUSD`, `ADAUSDT -> ADAUSD`) in `definitions-crypto.ts` and `biquoteProvider.supports` so BiQuote serves as an automatic high-quota (`15,000 req/min`) fallback when Binance hits its `20 req/min` self-throttle or regional IP blocks.
